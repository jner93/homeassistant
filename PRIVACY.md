# Privacy Policy — Home Assistant Nest Integration

**Last updated:** October 26, 2026

## Overview

Home Assistant Nest Integration is a personal-use integration that connects Google Nest smart home devices to a self-hosted Home Assistant instance.

The integration is intended exclusively for private, non-commercial smart home management by the owner and authorized household members.

## Information Accessed

With the account owner's authorization, the integration may access information made available through the Google Nest Smart Device Management (SDM) API, including:

- Nest device names, identifiers, and configuration information.
- Thermostat temperatures, setpoints, operating modes, and other supported device states.
- Device events and sensor information.
- Supported camera and doorbell events or streams, where applicable and authorized.

Access is limited to the permissions granted through Google's authorization process.

## How Information Is Used

Device information is used solely to display device status, provide smart home automations, and send authorized commands through Home Assistant.

The integration does not sell personal information, use Nest data for advertising, or use it to train artificial intelligence models.

## Data Storage and Security

Home Assistant operates on infrastructure controlled by the owner. Authentication credentials and device information are managed by Home Assistant and its configured integrations.

Home Assistant may store device states, historical data, logs, and backups according to the owner's configuration.

Communications with Google services occur through Google's authorized APIs and related services, including Google Cloud Pub/Sub where applicable.

## Data Sharing

The integration communicates with Google services as necessary to operate the authorized Nest integration.

It does not independently provide Nest user data to advertisers or data brokers.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Stored information is subject to Home Assistant's configured retention and backup settings and applicable Google Device Access requirements.

The account owner may revoke authorization through Google Account settings, remove the Nest integration from Home Assistant, and delete applicable stored information and backups.

## Third-Party Services

Google Nest devices and the Google SDM API are subject to Google's terms and privacy policies. Home Assistant is a separate open-source platform.

This personal integration is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google.

## Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes to the integration or its data practices.
